# Product direction

This file keeps durable product and operating constraints visible. It is the
sole authority for product names and for DM's public-source and privacy
boundary; do not restate either elsewhere.

## Current direction

- DM is the sole public portfolio agent. Nothing from the deleted
  implementation (#352/#353) is inherited; what ships now was rebuilt whole.
- DM's surface is a corner card that answers in the page and drives the site
  while it talks. It answers from a grounding corpus of the site's own approved
  facts, and it is honest instead of inventive: a refusal or a transport failure
  gets a short line and the direct email, never a canned pseudo-answer.
- Answers are generated by a DM service deployed outside this repository. The
  card only appears when that service is configured for a build; with no service
  configured, the Ask DM button opens the "being rebuilt" panel with a direct
  email link, and no request leaves the page.
- Eve and the Spotify-style player shell are retired product history and must
  not return as live seams.
- The portfolio remains recruiter-friendly, jargon-light, and static-first.
  Every page prerenders; core routes and content do not depend on client
  JavaScript or WebGL.

## Public-source and privacy boundary

Public answers may use only:

- the published project catalog in `src/data/catalog.ts`;
- static resume and contact data from `src/data/resume.ts`; and
- the owner-approved public profile in `src/data/profile.ts`.

Hidden drafts, private documents, private notes, candidate evidence, visitor
chats, credentials, and unsupported or generated claims are never public answer
sources. Profile text crosses the boundary only when its entry carries both
owner approval flags; an entry that loses either one disappears from public
output rather than degrading to a fallback. Career history crosses it only
through the public track allowlist. The DM runtime must enforce this boundary
and fail closed.

**Approved to use is not approved to publish.** The material above was cleared
as things DM may draw on when answering a question, not as a document anyone may
read end to end. The assembled corpus is therefore not served from this site at
any URL, is not sent to the browser, and has no public artifact. It leaves the
repository only as a snapshot the owner hands to the DM service's own
deployment. The browser receives only the page-action manifest — section anchors
and project ids, both already visible in the page's own HTML.

The boundary governs what feeds answers. It does not keep the conversation on
the site: a question typed into the card is sent, with the turns before it, to
the DM service — a separate deployment, on another origin, outside this
repository and outside this boundary's control. The card says so in plain words
where a visitor asks. Anything that visitor content is then used for, retained
in, or logged to is the service's responsibility to state and to bound.

## Runtime invariants

Any rebuilt DM runtime keeps rate limiting, request cancellation and deadlines,
and sanitized errors. Model and provider selection stays configurable outside
the repository; never commit secrets or provider configuration.

## Delivery and operations

- Agent-first redesign work targets `preview/agent-first-redesign`.
- One independently reviewable piece of work maps to one issue, branch, and
  linked pull request when tracked delivery is in use.
- Merge, deploy, promotion, publication, issue closure, provider changes, paid
  evaluations, and destructive cleanup require explicit gates.

## Deferred work

- Deploying the DM service and pointing a production build at it: an owner
  gate, not a code change. Until it is taken, DM answers nowhere but locally.
- Any reinstated publication workflow, scheduled refresh, or content-operations
  surface is new work, not a restoration — the previous implementations are
  deleted, not parked.
